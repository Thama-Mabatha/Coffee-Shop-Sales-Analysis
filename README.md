# ☕ Fourth Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

## 📊 Overview
This project analyzes **sales performance across multiple Fourth Coffee Shop locations** using **SQL, Excel, and Power BI**.  
The goal is to uncover trends in revenue, time-based performance, and product sales — enabling smarter decisions on **staffing, promotions, and operating hours**.

The analysis provides insights into:
- Revenue performance by store location  
- Sales trends by month, day, and time bucket (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)  
- Product performance by category and type  
- Actionable recommendations to improve profitability and efficiency  

---

## 🧠 Objectives
- Understand sales patterns across different times of day  
- Identify which store locations perform best  
- Analyze customer purchasing behavior by product category  
- Provide **data-driven recommendations** to increase revenue

## 📈 Key Analyses
- **Revenue by Store Location** – Comparison of monthly and total revenue across branches  
- **Sales by Month and Time Buckets** – Identifies when sales are highest (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)  
- **Total Sales by Product Category & Type** – Evaluates which product lines perform best  
- **Hourly Revenue Trends** – Highlights customer activity patterns throughout the day  

---

## 💡 Insights & Recommendations
- 🕗 **Morning hours (8 AM–10 AM)** generate the highest revenue — focus on quick service and breakfast specials  
- ☀️ **Sales drop after lunch** — introduce midday combo deals or loyalty rewards  
- 🌙 **Evening sales are lowest** — consider evening promotions or closing earlier to cut costs  
- 🏙️ **Lower Manhattan and Hell’s Kitchen** outperform other stores — review what drives their success (location, staffing, or marketing)
